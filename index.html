常見問題 Frequently Asked Questions

Panlo 是一款 AI 驅動的文件智能助理桌面應用程式。與一般文件管理工具不同，Panlo 會將您的文件進行向量化處理，讓您可以透過自然語言與文件對話、進行語意搜尋、自動生成摘要。它不只是儲存文件，而是讓文件變得「可對話」，成為您的智慧知識庫。 Panlo is an AI-powered document intelligence desktop app. Unlike regular file managers, Panlo vectorizes your documents so you can have natural language conversations with them, perform semantic searches, and auto-generate summaries. It doesn't just store files — it makes them "conversational," turning them into your intelligent knowledge base.

Panlo 會自動分析您的問題複雜度，並路由到最適合的 AI 模型：快速層處理簡單問答（2-5 秒）、平衡層處理多文件分析（5-10 秒）、推理層使用 AI 代理進行複雜的多步驟推理（15-30 秒）。這讓 80% 的日常查詢都能在幾秒內回覆，同時保留處理複雜分析的能力。 Panlo automatically analyzes your question's complexity and routes it to the optimal AI model: the Fast tier handles simple Q&A (2-5s), the Balanced tier handles multi-document analysis (5-10s), and the Reasoning tier uses AI agents for complex multi-step reasoning (15-30s). This means 80% of daily queries are answered in seconds, while retaining the power for complex analysis.

Panlo 支援 20+ 種檔案格式，包括：文件（PDF、DOC、DOCX、TXT、RTF）、試算表（XLS、XLSX、CSV）、簡報（PPT、PPTX）、圖片（JPEG、PNG、GIF、WEBP）、音訊（MP3、WAV、M4A、FLAC、AAC）、影片（MP4、MOV、AVI、MKV）、電子郵件（EML、MSG）以及程式碼等。音訊檔案會透過 Whisper 自動轉錄。 Panlo supports 20+ file formats including: documents (PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, RTF), spreadsheets (XLS, XLSX, CSV), presentations (PPT, PPTX), images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP), audio (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC), video (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV), emails (EML, MSG), and code files. Audio files are auto-transcribed via Whisper.
