智能代理工作流程是指由自主 AI 代理系統驅動的自動化業務流程。這些代理能夠理解複雜任務，自主規劃執行路徑，動態調整策略，並在多個代理之間協作完成複雜業務目標。與傳統自動化不同，代理系統具有決策能力和學習能力，能夠處理不確定性和異常情況。 Agentic workflow refers to automated business processes driven by autonomous AI agent systems. These agents can understand complex tasks, autonomously plan execution paths, dynamically adjust strategies, and collaborate across multiple agents to achieve complex business goals. Unlike traditional automation, agent systems have decision-making and learning capabilities, enabling them to handle uncertainty and exceptions.
